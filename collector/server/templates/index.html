Garmin Cycle Study

This study looks at how wearable health signals (heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep and stress) relate to the menstrual cycle, with the goal of detecting cycle phases and ovulation. To do this I need data from many people, so the patterns reflect real physiology and not just one body.

Your privacy. Your Garmin email and password are used only to download your health data, and are never stored — not on this server, not in any file, database or log. Your health data is saved under an anonymous ID (no name needed). Participation is voluntary.

Read full information & consent

What happens. When you submit this form, the server logs in to Garmin with the details you enter, downloads your health metrics (heart rate, HRV, sleep, stress, body battery, training and menstrual-cycle data) for roughly the past year, and saves them as a file labelled only with a random ID.

What we collect. The health metrics above, plus the few answers on this page (age range, contraception, cycle regularity, device, conditions). No name, no email, no Garmin password is stored.

How your login is handled. Your email and password are sent over a secure connection and used only at the moment of download. They are held in memory for those few seconds and then discarded — they are never written to disk or logs, and the researchers cannot see them or log in to your account afterwards.

Voluntary & withdrawal. Taking part is entirely voluntary. At the end you will see an anonymous ID — keep it. To withdraw your data later, email that ID to me at elsa.lopez-perez@inria.fr and I will delete it.
