Garmin Cycle Analytics - a women's health research project

This project is part of academic research on women's health and sports science. Its goal is to study menstrual cycle phases and ovulation using physiological signals collected from Garmin wearable devices with each participant's explicit consent.

What data we collect and how it is anonymised

With your consent, the study server signs in to your Garmin Connect account with the email and password you enter, and downloads your daily health summaries (resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep including overnight breathing and stress, and menstrual-cycle data) for roughly the past year. Your password is used only in memory for that sign-in and is never stored. Each participant is assigned a random identifier; no name, email address or Garmin account details are stored with the research data.

How we use the data

The data is used exclusively for non-commercial academic research. Results are analysed and published only in aggregated form; no individual participant's data is published or presented in a way that could identify them. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose.

Storage

The server runs on Render (a hosting provider) and stores nothing itself. Your data file is saved in an access-controlled Google Drive folder that only the research team can open. It is not sold or shared with any other party.

No use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) services

Your data is not shared with, processed by, or otherwise made available to any AI provider or AI service. All analysis and machine learning is performed locally by the research team using our own code; no participant data is sent to external AI systems such as large language models or hosted AI APIs.

Your rights

Participation is voluntary. You may withdraw at any time and request the complete deletion of all data associated with your participant identifier, without giving a reason. To exercise this right, or for any question about this policy, contact the research team at elsa.lopez-perez@inria.fr.
