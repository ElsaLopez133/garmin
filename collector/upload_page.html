Garmin Cycle Study

This study looks at how wearable health signals (heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep and stress) relate to the menstrual cycle, with the goal of detecting cycle phases and ovulation. To do this I need data from many people, so the patterns reflect real physiology and not just one body.

How to take part

  1. Go to Garmin account → Data Management → Export Your Data and click Request Data Export.
  2. Garmin emails you a download link (this can take a few days). Download the .zip file — you don't need to open it.
  3. Come back to this page, answer the questions below and choose that .zip file.

A computer works best, as the file can be large.

Your privacy. Your Garmin file is opened on your own device. Only daily health summaries are sent: sleep, heart rate, HRV, stress, body battery, training load, menstrual-cycle and lifestyle logs. Your GPS tracks, activities, profile, contacts and any free-text notes never leave your device. No Garmin password is needed. Your data is saved under an anonymous ID.

Read full information & consent

What happens. When you submit, this page reads your Garmin export in your browser, keeps only the daily health summary files listed above, removes your Garmin account and device identifiers and any free-text notes, and sends that reduced file to the research team, labelled only with a random ID.

What we collect. The daily health summaries above, for the whole period your Garmin account covers, plus the few answers on this page (age range, contraception, cycle regularity, device, conditions). No name, no email and no Garmin login are collected.

Voluntary & withdrawal. Taking part is entirely voluntary. At the end you will see an anonymous ID — keep it. To withdraw your data later, email that ID to me at elsa.lopez-perez@inria.fr and I will delete it.
