Garmin Cycle Analytics - a women's health research project

This project is part of academic research on women's health and sports science. Its goal is to study menstrual cycle phases and ovulation using physiological signals collected from Garmin wearable devices with each participant's explicit consent.

What data we collect and how it is anonymised

You provide the data yourself, using the data export that Garmin offers every user. Your export is opened on your own device; only the daily health summaries (sleep, heart rate, heart-rate variability, stress, body battery, training load, menstrual-cycle and lifestyle logs) are kept, and your Garmin account and device identifiers and any free-text notes are removed before anything is sent. Your activities, GPS tracks, profile and contacts never leave your device. Each participant is assigned a random identifier, and no personal identifiers such as name, email address or Garmin account details are stored with, or linked to, the research data. No Garmin login is requested.

How we use the data

The data is used exclusively for non-commercial academic research. Results are analysed and published only in aggregated form; no individual participant's data is published or presented in a way that could identify them. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose.

Storage and no sharing with third parties

The data is stored in an access-controlled Google Drive folder accessible only to the research team. It is not sold, shared with, or made available to any third party.

No use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) services

Your data is not shared with, processed by, or otherwise made available to any external AI provider, third-party AI service, or external data processing service. All analysis and machine learning is performed locally by the research team using our own code; no participant data is sent to external AI systems such as large language models or hosted AI APIs.

Your rights

Participation is voluntary. You may withdraw at any time and request the complete deletion of all data associated with your participant identifier, without giving a reason. To exercise this right, or for any question about this policy, contact the research team at elsa.lopez-perez@inria.fr.
