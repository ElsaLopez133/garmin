# Privacy Policy
*Garmin Cycle Analytics — a women's health research project*

This project is part of academic research at Inria on women's health and sports science. Its goal is to study menstrual cycle phases and ovulation using physiological signals (heart rate, HRV, sleep, stress, body battery, and related metrics) collected from Garmin wearable devices with each participant's explicit consent.

## What data we collect and how it is anonymised
With your consent, we retrieve your Garmin health and activity data (including data from the Health, Activity, and Women's Health APIs) for research purposes only. Data is anonymised at the point of collection: each participant is assigned a random identifier, and no personal identifiers (name, email address, Garmin account details, or credentials) are ever stored with, or linked to, the research data. Garmin login credentials are used solely to retrieve your data during collection and are never saved.

## How we use the data
The data is used exclusively for non-commercial academic research. Results are analysed and published only in aggregated form; no individual participant's data is ever published or presented in a way that could identify them. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose.

## No sharing with third parties
Data obtained via the Garmin APIs is not sold, shared with, or made available to any third party. It is stored on access-controlled research storage and is accessible only to the research team.

## No use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) services
Data obtained via the Garmin APIs is not shared with, processed by, or otherwise made available to any external AI provider, third-party AI service, or external data processing service. All analysis and machine learning is performed locally by the research team using our own code; no participant data is ever sent to external AI systems (such as large language models or hosted AI APIs).

## Your rights
Participation is voluntary. You may withdraw at any time and request the complete deletion of all data associated with your participant identifier, without giving a reason. To exercise this right, or for any question about this policy, contact the research team at elsa.lopez-perez@inria.fr.
